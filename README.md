## 🌍 Can Data Reveal How Universities Compete on the Global Stage?

University rankings offer more than just a list of the world's best institutions. They provide insights into academic performance, internationalisation, institutional strengths and changes in global higher education.

This project explores the **Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings dataset** from 2016 to 2023 through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The goal was to transform raw ranking data into meaningful visual insights and make comparisons across universities, countries and performance indicators easier to explore.

## 🔍 Explore the Dashboard

The dashboard is organised into four pages, each focusing on a different aspect of global higher education.

### 1. 🌐 Global University Rankings Analysis

**Which countries have the strongest representation in global university rankings?**

This page explores the geographical distribution of ranked universities and compares the number of institutions across countries.

Key highlights:
- United States leads with 1,288 ranked universities.
- United Kingdom follows with 765 universities.
- Japan ranks third with 727 universities.
- Interactive year and country filters allow users to explore changes over time.

![Global University Rankings Analysis](dashboard1_overview.png)

### 2. 📈 University Performance Overview

**How have leading universities performed over the years?**

This page tracks ranking trends among selected universities and examines changes in country representation from 2016 to 2023.

Key highlights:
- Compares ranking trends for Caltech, Harvard, Stanford, Cambridge and Oxford.
- Explores university representation across selected countries.
- Allows users to examine trends for individual universities.
- Oxford maintained the top position from 2017 to 2023 among the universities displayed.

![University Performance Overview](dashboard2_ranking_trend.png)

### 3. 🏛️ University Score Component Analysis

**What factors are reflected in university ranking performance?**

This page examines selected university performance indicators and their relationship with overall ranking positions.

Key highlights:
- Compares Teaching, Research and Industry Income scores over time.
- Explores changes in overall ranking positions.
- Includes a gender distribution analysis.
- Provides a university selector for more focused comparisons.

![University Score Component Analysis](dashboard3_score_analysis.png)

### 4. 🌏 International Student Growth

**Is internationalisation becoming more prominent in higher education?**

This page explores international student trends and international outlook scores across the selected period.

Key highlights:
- International student numbers increased from approximately 2.0 million in 2016 to 3.6 million in 2023.
- Compares international student growth across continents.
- Examines universities with higher percentages of international students.
- Tracks changes in average International Outlook scores.

![International Student Growth](dashboard4_international_growth.png)

## 💡 Key Insights Discovered

The dashboard highlights several interesting patterns in global higher education:

| Area | Observation |
|---|---|
| Global representation | The United States had the highest number of ranked universities, followed by the United Kingdom and Japan. |
| University rankings | Oxford ranked first from 2017 to 2023 among the selected universities displayed. |
| University performance | Teaching, Research and Industry Income scores provide different perspectives on institutional performance. |
| Internationalisation | International student numbers showed consistent growth throughout the analysis period. |
| International outlook | Average International Outlook scores fluctuated over the eight years, reaching their lowest displayed value in 2022 before increasing in 2023. |
